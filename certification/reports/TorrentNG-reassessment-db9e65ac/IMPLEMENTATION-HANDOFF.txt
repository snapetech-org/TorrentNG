BOUNDED POOL EXPERIMENT, NOT AN ACCEPTED WIN
experimental-bounded-512k-pool.patch adds the missing 512 KiB class with at most two retained idle buffers in that class. It leaves existing global byte limits unchanged. It is not applied to main.
The actual-current-storage probe showed ~91% fewer allocated bytes but nearly unchanged allocation event count. CPU change was small with overlapping ranges. The class-specific cap matters: without it, post-scheduler idle pool retention was ~9.39 MiB; capped it was ~1.89 MiB, versus ~0.89 MiB baseline.
The two-buffer choice is exploratory. Validate peer bursts, partial completions, frame-cap denial, last-alias lifetimes, zeroing and idle retention on the actual daemon before accepting it. Three focused invariants passed in this audit; the full workspace was not rerun here.
