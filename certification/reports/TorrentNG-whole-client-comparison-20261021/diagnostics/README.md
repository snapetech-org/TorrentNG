# Excluded setup and preflight attempts

These files retain earlier controller, helper-order, socket-path, source-image, and precommit/prebranding attempts made while preparing the three complete seed rounds. They are kept for failure analysis and are excluded from the comparison table. Use only the `seed-main-round1`, `seed-main-round2`, and `seed-main-round3-latest` reports for the matched seed results.

The generated setup reports referenced generic sample names that were not present in the output directory. Their readable `.md` copies point to the attached sample/profile files; each original report is retained byte-for-byte with a `.md.raw` suffix, and its SHA-256 is listed in the artifact manifest.

The [stale-project log captures](stale-project-logs/README.md) preserve logs from one leftover isolated benchmark Compose project. Authentication-related lines in the qBittorrent logs are redacted; original-file SHA-256 digests and redaction counts are recorded in the artifact manifest.
